Can’t find what you need? We’re always happy to talk.

When can kittens go to their new homes?

Once they are developmentally ready, confidently socialised, and have received their scheduled care. We’ll confirm each kitten’s individual timeline with you.

What comes home with my kitten?

Each family receives care records, feeding guidance, and practical settling-in advice.

Can I reserve a kitten?

Yes. Start with a conversation so we can make sure the match feels right for everyone.

Do you offer ongoing support?

Absolutely. We love updates and remain available as your kitten grows.
